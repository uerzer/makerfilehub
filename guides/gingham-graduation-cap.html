The Zero-Weed Graduation Keepsake (Gingham Cap)

Every Cricut seller knows the pain of "weeding"—spending 20 minutes hunched over a desk with a sharp metal hook, trying to peel away tiny scraps of vinyl from an intricate design.

If you want to increase your hourly profit rate, you have to sell designs that require zero weeding. You need large, flat, geometric shapes.

The Gingham Graduation Cap

We designed a graduation cap SVG specifically for speed. It features a bold navy cap, a cream tassel, and a sage/ivory gingham ribbon. Because the shapes are large blocks, your Cricut cuts it in seconds, and assembly takes less than a minute.

The Cardstock Setup (Affiliate Links)

When making flat papercrafts, texture is everything. Since the shapes are simple, the paper needs to look premium.

  • The Base: Navy Blue Linen-Textured Cardstock
  • The Gingham: You can print your own pattern, or buy pre-printed Sage Gingham Scrapbook Paper.
  • The Dimension: Use 3M Double-Sided Foam Tape between the cap and the tassel to give the keepsake a 3D pop.
