The 'Strawberry Sweet' Baby Shower Trend is Taking Over 2026

Move over, boho rainbows. The breakout baby shower theme for Spring/Summer 2026 is "Strawberry Sweet"—a mix of vintage red strawberries, crisp white-and-sage gingham, and retro scalloped edges.

If you are a Cricut seller, you need this in your shop right now. The search volume for strawberry-themed shower favors is spiking, and the supply of high-quality cut files hasn't caught up.

The Money Maker: The Gingham Strawberry Cake Topper

Party decor is where Cricut makers clean up. A custom acrylic or layered cardstock cake topper sells for $15-$25 on Etsy, and costs pennies to make.

Why this design converts:

It uses a 2x2 gingham pattern base with a flat, geometric strawberry overlay. There are no gradient meshes or microscopic seeds to weed. We designed the SVG specifically to keep the cut paths clean and thick enough that your machine won't chew up the paper.
