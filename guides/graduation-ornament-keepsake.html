Graduation

The 2026 Graduation Keepsake Every Grandparent is Buying

If you run a Cricut shop, you know the graduation wave hits twice: first when the seniors order their shirts, and second when the grandparents order the keepsakes.

This year, the trend has shifted away from generic "Class of 2026" mugs. Grandparents want heirlooms. They want personalized, timeless pieces that look expensive but take you less than 10 minutes to cut and press.

The Winning Design: The Sage & Gold Keepsake Ornament

We’ve watched this specific design—a flat, sage-green oval with a gold band and a personalized heart emblem—dominate early Etsy search volume. It bridges the gap between "handmade" and "high-end boutique."

Why this works for your shop:

  • High Perceived Value: Grandparents routinely pay $22+ for personalized ornaments.
  • Low Material Cost: You need less than $2 in cardstock and vinyl.
  • Fast Production: It’s 3 flat layers. No weeding tiny cursive fonts.
